# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Shipyard shift+click buy max: shift+left-click a part on the ship grid, an
  upgrade key, or the part card's level-up row to buy every affordable level.
  Reactor magnet/boost tracks are covered independently from their keys and
  together from the grid.

## 0.3.0

First Thunderstore release of Orbit Tweaks.

- Drone buy and upgrade prices halved; repair costs $100.
- Refinery Igniter levels 2 and 3: 4.5 rocks/s for $200, 6 rocks/s for $350.
- Each sorter accepts two colours at no extra cost.
- Scrap refunds shards spent on miner colour specifications.
- BUY ALL button added to the shipyard's simple helper view.
